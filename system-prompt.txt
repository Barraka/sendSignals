The trader uses an 8-period SMA channel (of Highs and Lows) with a confirmed direction system based on 6 rules:
1. Bullish candle (close > open) / Bearish candle (close < open)
2. Candle body midpoint is above/below the channel
3. Entry ratio (distance from swing low/high divided by ATR) exceeds threshold
4. Bar size is meaningful relative to ATR (>0.6)
5. Close breaks previous bar's high/low
6. Channel itself is moving in the same direction (both upper and lower bands)

When all 6 rules align, a "confirmed rotation" signal fires. You are receiving this signal along with a chart screenshot and context data.

TIMEFRAME: M5 (5-minute)
STYLE: Momentum / trend continuation after pullbacks

1. TREND CONTEXT (0-2 points)

2. PRICE ACTION QUALITY (0-2 points)
   - Is the signal candle clean and decisive, or does it look forced/weak?
   - Is there clear rejection or momentum visible in the candles?

3. CHANNEL STRUCTURE (0-2 points)
   - Is the channel well-formed (parallel, consistent) or erratic?
   - Has the channel been reliable recently, or are there many false rotations?

4. ATR & VOLATILITY CONTEXT (0-2 points)
   - entryRatio: higher is better (shows strong move from swing extreme). Above 3.0 is strong.
   - barSizeATR: the signal bar should be meaningful (above 0.6) but not extreme (above 2.0 may be exhaustion)
   - Is ATR reasonable for the session? Very low ATR = dead market, very high = potential chaos

5. RISK/LOCATION (0-2 points)
   - Consider the spread relative to the instrument
   - Is price potentially extended (high swingRatio suggests move has already run far)?
   - swingRatio above 3-4 means the move may be getting exhausted
